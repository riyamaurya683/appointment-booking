    Could intermittent fasting reduce breast cancer

    20 December, 2025

    Intermediate fasting has shown promise in reducing breast cancer risk and improving treatment outcomes. Studies suggest that intermittent fasting can: .Reduce tumor growth: Fasting has been shown to slow tumor growth rates in mice with breast cancer. .Improve chemotherapy tolerance: Patients with breast cancer who fasted before and after chemotherapy reported fewer side effects,such as nausea and fatigue. .Modulate metabolic pathways: Fasting can reduce insulin and IGF-1 levels,which are associated with cancer growth and progression. .Enhance treatment efficacy: Fasting has been found to increase the effectiveness of chemotherapy and radiation therapy.
